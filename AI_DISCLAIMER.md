# AI Usage Notice

## AI involvement

MeditorD is built from human ideas, with generative AI supporting design, research, implementation, testing, and documentation throughout development.

## Human oversight and responsibility

The project maintainer is responsible for the final decisions and published work. AI-assisted contributions should be reviewed and tested before relying on them.

## Limitations and attribution

AI assistance does not independently guarantee accuracy, reliability, or security. For project references and third-party material, see [RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md) and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
